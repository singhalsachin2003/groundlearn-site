BA II Plus annuity due: BGN mode keystrokes and the trap

Leases, rent, insurance premiums and retirement withdrawals that begin today.

The keys

2NDBGN2NDSETToggles to BGN mode. A small BGN appears on screen.
2NDQUITBack to the standard display, still in BGN mode.
… solve as usual …Each payment now arrives one period earlier.
2NDBGN2NDSETSwitch back to END when done.

Worked example

The same five payments of 1,000 at 8%, but paid at the start of each year.

2NDBGN2NDSET2NDQUITBGN on.
5N8I/Y1000PMT0FVSame inputs.
CPTPVPV = −4,312.13.
PV = −4,312.13, which is the ordinary annuity × 1.08

The trap

BGN mode stays on until you switch it off — even after turning the calculator off. Check for the BGN indicator before every TVM problem.

The terms behind it

  • AnnuityA series of equal cash flows at equal intervals.
  • Present valueWhat a future cash flow is worth today, found by discounting it at a rate that reflects its risk and timing.
