BA II Plus effective annual rate: the ICONV worksheet

Comparing quoted rates with different compounding, and EAR questions.

The keys

2NDICONVOpens the interest conversion worksheet.
rateENTER↓NOM is the stated annual rate.
↓periodsENTERC/Y is compounding periods per year.
↑CPTComputes EFF.

Worked example

A bank quotes 6% compounded quarterly. What is the effective annual rate?

2NDICONVOpen the worksheet.
6ENTER↓NOM = 6.
↓4ENTERC/Y = 4.
↑CPTEFF = 6.136.
EFF = 6.136%

The trap

Entering EFF and computing NOM works too, which is useful when a question gives an effective rate and needs a periodic one.

The terms behind it

  • Effective annual rateThe rate that, compounded once a year, gives the same growth as a stated nominal rate compounded more often.
  • Continuous compoundingCompounding over infinitely small intervals.
