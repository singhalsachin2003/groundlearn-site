BA II Plus keystrokes for CFA and FRM

Eight keystroke walkthroughs for the financial calculator most CFA® and FRM® candidates take into the exam, each with a worked example and the trap that catches people.

The eight walkthroughs

  • How to set up the BA II Plus for the CFA and FRM examsOnce, before you start practising — and again if the calculator is reset.
  • BA II Plus time value of money (TVM): keystrokes and a worked exampleAnnuities, loans, bond prices and retirement problems in every level.
  • BA II Plus annuity due: BGN mode keystrokes and the trapLeases, rent, insurance premiums and retirement withdrawals that begin today.
  • BA II Plus NPV and IRR: cash flow worksheet keystrokesCapital budgeting, private equity cash flows and money-weighted returns.
  • BA II Plus effective annual rate: the ICONV worksheetComparing quoted rates with different compounding, and EAR questions.
  • BA II Plus loan amortization: interest and principal for any yearMortgage payments, interest and principal splits, and outstanding balances.
  • BA II Plus mean and standard deviation: the DATA and STAT worksheetsSample statistics on returns in Quantitative Methods.
  • BA II Plus bond price from yield: keystrokes and the semi-annual trapFixed income pricing at every level, using the TVM keys.

Four traps examiners count on

  • P/Y left at 12. Every annual rate is quietly divided by twelve. Set P/Y to 1 once.
  • BGN left on. It survives switching the calculator off. Look for the small BGN on screen.
  • The minus key instead of +/−. Minus subtracts; +/− makes a number negative.
  • A worksheet never cleared. Old cash flows or data points silently change the answer. Clear first, every time.
