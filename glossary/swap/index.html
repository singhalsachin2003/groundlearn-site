Swap

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

An agreement to exchange one stream of cash flows for another over time.

The exam angle

A plain vanilla interest rate swap is a series of forward rate agreements, and can also be read as being long one bond and short another.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • DerivativesCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Forwards, futures, swaps, options and arbitrage-free pricing.
  • Financial Markets & ProductsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · Institutions, market structure, futures, swaps, options, rates, FX, MBS.

Related terms

  • Basis riskThe risk that a hedge and the exposure it hedges do not move together, leaving a residual.
  • Contango and backwardationContango is a futures price above the spot price; backwardation is a futures price below it.
  • DerivativeA contract whose value depends on the price of something else — an asset, a rate, an index or an event.
  • Forward contractA private agreement to buy or sell an asset at a set price on a future date.
  • Futures contractA standardised, exchange-traded forward, marked to market daily through a clearing house.
