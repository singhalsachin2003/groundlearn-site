Yield curve strategy

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Positioning a bond portfolio for an expected change in the shape of the curve — bullet, barbell or ladder structures expressing different views.

The exam angle

A barbell outperforms a duration-matched bullet when the curve flattens, and has more convexity. Duration alone cannot express a curve view.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Portfolio Management pathwayCFA Level III · exam weight 30–35% · Active equity, yield-curve and credit strategy, LDI, trade execution.
  • Fixed IncomeCFA Level II · exam weight 10–15% · Term structure dynamics, arbitrage-free valuation, embedded options, CDS.
