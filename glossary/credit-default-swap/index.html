Credit default swap (CDS)

Also called CDS. A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

A contract in which the buyer pays a periodic premium and receives compensation if a specified borrower suffers a credit event.

The exam angle

Buying protection is economically like shorting the credit. The spread is a market-implied view of default probability and recovery together, not of default alone.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • DerivativesCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Forwards, futures, swaps, options and arbitrage-free pricing.
  • Credit Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.
