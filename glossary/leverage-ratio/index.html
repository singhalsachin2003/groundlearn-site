Leverage ratio

A FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Capital divided by total exposure, without risk weighting — a backstop to the risk-weighted requirement.

The exam angle

Exists because risk weights can be gamed and because a model that says an exposure is riskless has been wrong before.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Current Issues in Financial MarketsFRM Part II · exam weight 10% · This cycle's readings — AI in finance, climate risk, digital assets, bank stress.

  • Economic capitalThe capital a firm judges it needs to absorb unexpected losses at a chosen confidence level, computed internally.
  • Liquidity coverage ratioThe requirement that a bank holds enough high-quality liquid assets to survive a thirty-day stress scenario.
  • Model riskThe risk of loss from using a model that is wrong, or right but used outside the conditions it was built for.
