Credit spread

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The extra yield a borrower pays over a risk-free benchmark of the same maturity, compensating for default and liquidity risk.

The exam angle

Widening spreads hurt even when the issuer never defaults, and spread duration measures that exposure separately from rate duration.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Fixed IncomeCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Pricing, yield measures, duration, convexity, credit spreads.
  • Credit Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.

Related terms

  • ConvexityThe curvature of the price–yield relationship — how duration itself changes as yields move.
