Prepayment risk

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The risk that borrowers repay mortgage principal early, most often when rates fall and refinancing becomes attractive.

The exam angle

The cause of negative convexity in MBS: exactly when a normal bond would rally, the cash flows come back to be reinvested at the new lower rate.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Fixed IncomeCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Pricing, yield measures, duration, convexity, credit spreads.
  • Credit Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.
