Business cycle

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The recurring pattern of expansion, peak, contraction and trough in economic activity.

The exam angle

Sector rotation questions follow directly from cycle position — defensives late, cyclicals early.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • EconomicsCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Market structures, business cycles, policy, trade and exchange rates.
