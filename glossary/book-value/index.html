Book value

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The accounting value of equity: assets less liabilities as reported.

The exam angle

Price-to-book works best where assets are marked near fair value — banks — and worst where value is intangible.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • EquitiesCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Market organisation, indexes, industry analysis, dividend models.
  • Financial Statement AnalysisCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · The three statements, inventories, long-lived assets, quality of earnings.

Related terms

  • Earnings per shareNet income available to common shareholders, divided by the weighted average shares outstanding.
  • EBITDAEarnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation — a rough proxy for operating cash generation.
  • Free cash flowCash available after the investment needed to maintain the business.
  • Return on equityNet income as a percentage of average shareholders’ equity.
  • BetaThe sensitivity of an asset’s return to the market’s return — its share of systematic risk.
  • Capital asset pricing modelA model stating that an asset’s expected return equals the risk-free rate plus beta times the equity risk premium.
