Implied volatility

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The volatility that, put into an option pricing model, returns the option’s observed market price.

The exam angle

A price quoted in volatility units rather than a forecast. Plotting it against strike gives the smile or skew.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • DerivativesCFA Level II · exam weight 5–10% · Forward commitments and contingent claims — binomial, BSM, the Greeks.
  • Valuation & Risk ModelsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · VaR and ES, option valuation, bond risk, stress testing, credit and op risk.
  • Market Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.

Related terms

  • GammaThe rate at which delta changes as the underlying moves.
  • Black–Scholes–Merton modelA closed-form model for European option prices, assuming lognormal prices, constant volatility and continuous…
  • Delta hedgingOffsetting an option position with the underlying so the combined position is insensitive to small price moves.
  • RhoThe sensitivity of an option’s value to a change in the risk-free rate.
  • ThetaThe change in an option’s value from the passage of one day, all else equal.
  • VegaThe change in an option’s value for a one point change in implied volatility.
