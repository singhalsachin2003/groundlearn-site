Put–call parity

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The no-arbitrage relationship linking a European call, a European put, the underlying and a risk-free bond of the same strike and expiry.

c + PV(X) = p + S₀

The exam angle

Rearranging it constructs synthetic positions, and a violation is a textbook arbitrage. European options only.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • DerivativesCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Forwards, futures, swaps, options and arbitrage-free pricing.
  • Valuation & Risk ModelsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · VaR and ES, option valuation, bond risk, stress testing, credit and op risk.

Related terms

  • DeltaThe change in an option’s value for a small change in the underlying price.
  • Lognormal distributionThe distribution of a variable whose natural logarithm is normally distributed.
  • Put optionThe right, without the obligation, to sell an asset at the strike price by expiry.
  • Basis riskThe risk that a hedge and the exposure it hedges do not move together, leaving a residual.
