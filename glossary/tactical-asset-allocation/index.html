Tactical asset allocation (TAA)

Also called TAA. A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Short-term deliberate deviation from the strategic allocation to exploit a perceived opportunity.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Asset AllocationCFA Level III · exam weight 15–20% · Capital market expectations, allocation principles, real-world constraints.

Related terms

  • Currency hedgingUsing forwards, futures or options to reduce a portfolio’s exposure to exchange rate movements on foreign assets.
  • Efficient frontierThe set of portfolios offering the highest expected return for each level of risk.
  • ImmunisationStructuring a bond portfolio so that price and reinvestment effects cancel, locking in a return over a horizon.
  • Liability-driven investingManaging a portfolio against the liabilities it must fund rather than against a market index.
  • RebalancingTrading back to target weights after market moves have shifted them.
  • Reinvestment riskThe risk that coupons and principal must be reinvested at a lower rate than expected.
