Delta

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The change in an option’s value for a small change in the underlying price.

The exam angle

Ranges 0 to 1 for a call and −1 to 0 for a put. Roughly the hedge ratio, and loosely read as the risk-neutral probability of finishing in the money.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • DerivativesCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Forwards, futures, swaps, options and arbitrage-free pricing.
  • Valuation & Risk ModelsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · VaR and ES, option valuation, bond risk, stress testing, credit and op risk.
  • Market Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.
