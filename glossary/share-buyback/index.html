Share repurchase

Also called buyback. A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

A company buying its own shares, reducing the share count.

The exam angle

Equivalent to a dividend in cash terms but more flexible and often tax-advantaged. Raises EPS mechanically, which is not the same as creating value.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Corporate FinanceCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Governance, working capital, capital allocation, capital structure.

Related terms

  • Agency problemThe conflict that arises when managers act in their own interest rather than that of the owners who employ them.
  • Business riskThe uncertainty in operating income arising from the nature of the business itself, before any financing decision.
  • Capital budgetingThe process of deciding which long-term investments a company should make.
  • Capital structureThe mix of debt and equity a company uses to fund itself.
  • Cash conversion cycleThe time between paying for inventory and collecting cash from the customer.
  • Corporate governanceThe system of controls by which a company is directed and held accountable to its stakeholders.
