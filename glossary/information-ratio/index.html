Information ratio

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Active return divided by tracking error — value added per unit of benchmark-relative risk.

The exam angle

The natural measure for a benchmarked active manager, where the Sharpe ratio would mostly reflect the benchmark.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Performance MeasurementCFA Level III · exam weight 5–10% · Attribution, appraisal, manager selection and the GIPS standards.
  • Portfolio ConstructionCFA Level I · exam weight 8–12% · Risk and return, the CAPM, IPS construction, behavioural biases.
