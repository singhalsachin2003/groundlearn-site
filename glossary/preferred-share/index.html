Preference share

Also called preferred stock. A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

A share with a stated dividend that ranks ahead of common equity but behind all debt, usually without a vote.

The exam angle

Economically a hybrid. Cumulative preference shares accrue missed dividends; non-cumulative do not.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • EquitiesCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Market organisation, indexes, industry analysis, dividend models.

Related terms

  • BetaThe sensitivity of an asset’s return to the market’s return — its share of systematic risk.
  • Book valueThe accounting value of equity: assets less liabilities as reported.
  • Capital asset pricing modelA model stating that an asset’s expected return equals the risk-free rate plus beta times the equity risk premium.
  • Common shareAn ownership claim on a company’s residual cash flows and assets, normally carrying a vote and ranking last in…
  • Cost of equityThe return equity holders require for bearing the residual risk of the business.
  • Dividend discount modelValuing a share as the present value of its expected future dividends.
