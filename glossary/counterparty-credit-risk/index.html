Counterparty credit risk

A FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The risk that the other side of a derivative defaults before settling, when the contract has positive value to you.

The exam angle

Two-sided and stochastic, unlike a loan: exposure moves with the market, so it must be modelled over time rather than measured once.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Credit Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.
  • Financial Markets & ProductsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · Institutions, market structure, futures, swaps, options, rates, FX, MBS.
