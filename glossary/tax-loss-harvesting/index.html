Tax-loss harvesting

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Realising losses deliberately to offset gains elsewhere, while keeping the portfolio’s economic exposure broadly intact.

The exam angle

Defers tax rather than avoiding it, because the replacement asset carries a lower basis. Wash-sale rules restrict repurchasing the same security.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Private Wealth pathwayCFA Level III · exam weight 30–35% · Goals-based planning, tax efficiency, human capital, wealth transfer.

Related terms

  • Asset locationDeciding which account type holds which asset, so that heavily taxed returns sit in tax-sheltered accounts.
  • Goals-based investingStructuring wealth into separate portfolios for separate objectives, each with its own horizon and risk tolerance,…
  • Human capitalThe present value of an individual’s expected future earnings, treated as an asset alongside their financial wealth.
  • Wealth transferMoving assets to the next generation or to charity, structured for tax efficiency and for the family’s intentions.
