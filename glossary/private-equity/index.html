Private equity

A CFA term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Equity investment in companies that are not publicly traded, typically through a closed-end fund with a fixed life.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Alternative InvestmentsCFA Level I · exam weight 6–9% · Private capital, real estate, natural resources, hedge funds, digital assets.
  • Alternative InvestmentsCFA Level II · exam weight 5–10% · Commodities, real estate direct and listed, hedge fund strategies.

Related terms

  • Hedge fundA privately offered pooled vehicle with broad freedom over strategy, leverage and short selling.
  • Leveraged buyoutAcquiring a company using a large proportion of borrowed money, secured against the target’s own assets and cash flows.
  • Capital callThe general partner’s demand that limited partners transfer part of their committed capital.
  • Capitalisation rateNet operating income divided by property value — a property’s unlevered income yield.
  • Carried interestThe general partner’s share of a private fund’s profits, typically around 20%.
  • Contango and backwardationContango is a futures price above the spot price; backwardation is a futures price below it.
