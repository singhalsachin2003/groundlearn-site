Continuous compounding

A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Compounding over infinitely small intervals. The limit of periodic compounding as the number of periods per year goes to infinity.

FV = PV × e^(r × t)

The exam angle

The default convention in derivatives pricing and in most FRM formulas, which is why the same position can look differently priced in two parts of the curriculum.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Quantitative MethodsCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Returns and benchmarks, time value, distributions, portfolio maths, regression.
  • Quantitative AnalysisFRM Part I · exam weight 20% · Probability, hypothesis testing, regression, time series, volatility, simulation.
  • Valuation & Risk ModelsFRM Part I · exam weight 30% · VaR and ES, option valuation, bond risk, stress testing, credit and op risk.
