Market liquidity risk

A FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The risk that a position cannot be sold quickly without moving the price against you.

The exam angle

Measured through bid–ask spread, market depth and resilience. Liquidity-adjusted VaR adds the cost of exiting to the price risk.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Liquidity & Treasury RiskFRM Part II · exam weight 15% · Funding vs market liquidity, LVaR, transfer pricing, IRRBB, LCR and NSFR.
  • Market Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.
