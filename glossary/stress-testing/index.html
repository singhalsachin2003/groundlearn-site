Stress testing

A FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

Revaluing a portfolio under severe but plausible scenarios, rather than under the distribution of ordinary days.

The exam angle

Complements VaR instead of competing with it: VaR describes the usual, stress testing the unusual. Reverse stress testing starts from the failure and asks what would cause it.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Market Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.
  • Current Issues in Financial MarketsFRM Part II · exam weight 10% · This cycle's readings — AI in finance, climate risk, digital assets, bank stress.
  • Foundations of Risk ManagementFRM Part I · exam weight 20% · Risk taxonomy, governance, CAPM, multifactor models, financial disasters.
