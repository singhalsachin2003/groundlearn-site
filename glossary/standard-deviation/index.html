Standard deviation

Also called volatility, vol, sigma. A CFA and FRM term, defined in plain English with the distinction examiners test.

What it means

The square root of variance, in the same units as the data. In finance it is the usual proxy for total risk.

The exam angle

Penalises upside and downside equally, which is its main criticism and the motivation for downside measures like semi-deviation and VaR.

Where it appears in the syllabus

  • Quantitative MethodsCFA Level I · exam weight 11–14% · Returns and benchmarks, time value, distributions, portfolio maths, regression.
  • Portfolio ConstructionCFA Level I · exam weight 8–12% · Risk and return, the CAPM, IPS construction, behavioural biases.
  • Quantitative AnalysisFRM Part I · exam weight 20% · Probability, hypothesis testing, regression, time series, volatility, simulation.
  • Market Risk Measurement & ManagementFRM Part II · exam weight 20% · VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.
