FRM Part II topic weights

6 topic areas, ranked by weight, with the modules, key terms and practice links for each.

The weights at a glance

80 equally weighted multiple-choice questions in four hours, by computer-based testing. Offered in May, August and November.

What each topic covers

Ordered from the heaviest weight down. Module names follow the official outline.

Market Risk Measurement & Management

20% of the exam

VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.

  • VaR estimation approaches and their limitations; backtesting VaR
  • Expected Shortfall and other coherent measures; parametric and non-parametric estimation
  • Extreme value theory and tail risk
  • Volatility modelling: GARCH, EWMA, implied volatility surfaces
  • Correlation and copulas
  • Fixed-income and term-structure models; interest rate risk hedging
  • Volatility smiles; exotic and mortgage-backed products
  • Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB)

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Standard deviation · Correlation · Kurtosis · Monte Carlo simulation · Carry trade · Duration · all 25. Worked guides: Expected shortfall versus VaR · VaR and the square-root-of-time rule. Videos: Expected shortfall vs VaR (2:36) · VaR and the square-root-of-time rule (2:43).

Credit Risk Measurement & Management

20% of the exam

Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.

  • Credit analysis and the credit process; ratings and rating transitions
  • Structural and reduced-form default models; Merton model
  • Portfolio credit risk, default correlation and credit VaR
  • Counterparty risk, CVA/DVA/FVA and wrong-way risk
  • Netting, collateral and margining
  • Credit derivatives and credit default swaps
  • Securitisation, structured credit and CDOs
  • Basel credit risk capital frameworks; stress testing credit portfolios

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Adverse selection · Interest coverage ratio · Credit spread · Securitisation · Tranche · Prepayment risk · all 18. Worked guides: Credit valuation adjustment (CVA): a worked example · Funding valuation adjustment (FVA). Videos: Credit valuation adjustment (CVA), worked example (2:14) · Funding valuation adjustment (FVA), worked example (2:58).

Operational Risk & Resilience

20% of the exam

RCSA and loss data, model risk, cyber, third parties, financial crime, Basel.

  • Operational risk taxonomy, RCSA and loss data collection
  • Modelling operational risk loss distributions; capital approaches
  • Risk governance, risk appetite and the three lines of defence
  • Model risk management and validation
  • Cyber risk and information security; third-party and outsourcing risk
  • Business continuity and operational resilience
  • Financial crime: money laundering, sanctions and fraud
  • Basel operational risk framework; regulation and supervision
  • Stress testing and enterprise risk management

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Operational risk · Rogue trading · Model risk. Worked guides: Barings, 1995 · Société Générale, 2008.

Liquidity & Treasury Risk

15% of the exam

Funding vs market liquidity, LVaR, transfer pricing, IRRBB, LCR and NSFR.

  • Liquidity risk: funding vs market liquidity
  • Liquidity-adjusted VaR and transaction cost measurement
  • Cash flow modelling and liquidity stress testing
  • Intraday, collateral and contingent liquidity management
  • Deposit and wholesale funding structures; transfer pricing
  • Balance sheet management; repo markets
  • Interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB)
  • LCR, NSFR and the Basel liquidity framework
  • Contingency funding plans

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Initial and variation margin · Lock-up period · Illiquidity premium · Collateral · Funding liquidity risk · Market liquidity risk · all 11. Worked guides: Funding valuation adjustment (FVA) · The gilt LDI crisis, 2022. Videos: Funding valuation adjustment (FVA), worked example (2:58).

Risk & Investment Management

15% of the exam

Factor theory, risk budgeting, attribution, hedge funds, illiquid assets.

  • Factor theory and portfolio construction
  • Risk budgeting and risk monitoring for investment portfolios
  • Portfolio performance measurement and attribution
  • Hedge fund strategies, structures and risks
  • Illiquid assets and valuation of private holdings
  • Risk-adjusted performance measures
  • Portfolio VaR and risk decomposition

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Multifactor model · Factor investing · Arbitrage pricing theory · Risk budgeting · Marginal VaR · Performance attribution · all 10.

Current Issues in Financial Markets

10% of the exam

This cycle's readings — AI in finance, climate risk, digital assets, bank stress.

  • A rotating set of readings, replaced substantially each year
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning in finance
  • Climate and transition risk
  • Digital assets and stablecoins
  • Cyber and operational resilience
  • Post-2023 banking-stress lessons

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Moral hazard · Stress testing · Model risk · Bank run · Basel Accords · Liquidity coverage ratio · all 12.
