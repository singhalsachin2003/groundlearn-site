How we teach

Foundations first. Then practice that sticks.

Every app we make follows the same four rules.

Small steps

Lessons and sessions take minutes, not hours. One idea, one example, one question, so a spare ten minutes counts.

Recall over rereading

You are asked questions, not just shown answers. Anything you miss goes into a review queue and comes back at the right time.

Works offline

Study on a commute or a flight. Content lives on your phone, so there is nothing to wait for and nothing to buffer.

Private and ad-free

No ads, no feeds and no account needed to begin. Our apps exist to teach, not to hold your attention.

About

An independent studio for serious self-study.

Groundlearn is a small, independent studio. It is run by one person who has worked in derivatives valuation and risk for many years and wanted better ways to learn the material than long slide decks and thick manuals.

The goal is simple: take subjects that are hard to start, break them into steps that fit into a day, and make sure what you learn stays learned. More subjects will follow Cornerstone and OTC Learn.

Questions, corrections and ideas are welcome at contactgroundlearn@gmail.com.

Educational content only. Nothing here is financial advice, an offer to trade, or a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument.
