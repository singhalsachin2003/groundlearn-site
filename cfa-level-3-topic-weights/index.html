CFA Level III topic weights for 2027

5 topic areas plus a pathway, ranked by weight, with the modules, key terms and practice links for each.

The weights at a glance

Vignette-based essay and multiple-choice questions over 4.5 hours, in two sessions of 132 minutes, with one of three specialised pathways. Offered in February and August.

Topic areaWeightRelative size
Asset Allocation15–20%

Weights are the published bands from CFA Institute's 2027 topic outlines, checked in October 2026. The midpoint is shown only to rank topics.

What each topic covers

Ordered from the heaviest weight down. Module names follow the official outline.

Asset Allocation

15–20% of the exam

Capital market expectations, allocation principles, real-world constraints.

  • Capital Market Expectations, Part 1: Framework and Macro Considerations
  • Capital Market Expectations, Part 2: Forecasting Asset Class Returns
  • Overview of Asset Allocation
  • Principles of Asset Allocation
  • Asset Allocation with Real-World Constraints

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Reinvestment risk · Immunisation · Efficient frontier · Strategic asset allocation · Tactical asset allocation · Rebalancing · all 9.

Portfolio Construction

15–20% of the exam

Equity and fixed-income portfolios, alternatives, institutions, trading costs.

  • Overview of Equity Portfolio Management
  • Overview of Fixed-Income Portfolio Management
  • Asset Allocation to Alternative Investments
  • An Overview of Private Wealth Management
  • Portfolio Management for Institutional Investors
  • Trading Costs and Electronic Markets
  • Case Study in Portfolio Management: Institutional (SWF)

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Suitability · Tracking error · Investment policy statement · Risk tolerance · Liability-driven investing · Behavioural bias · all 8.

Derivatives & Risk Management

10–15% of the exam

Options strategies, swaps/forwards/futures strategies, currency management.

  • Options Strategies
  • Swaps, Forwards, and Futures Strategies
  • Currency Management: An Introduction

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Covered call · Protective put · Collar · Straddle · Currency hedging · Derivatives overlay.

Ethical & Professional Standards

10–15% of the exam

The Standards plus the Asset Manager Code — which appears only at Level III.

  • Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct
  • Guidance for Standards I–VII (seven separate modules)
  • Application of the Code and Standards: Level III
  • Asset Manager Code of Professional Conduct

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: Code of Ethics · Standards of Professional Conduct · Fiduciary duty · Suitability · Investment policy statement.

Performance Measurement

5–10% of the exam

Attribution, appraisal, manager selection and the GIPS standards.

  • Portfolio Performance Evaluation
  • Investment Manager Selection
  • Overview of the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS)

Five practice questions with answers · Topic map. Key terms: GIPS · Composite · Money-weighted return · Time-weighted return · Alpha · Sharpe ratio · all 11.

The three pathways

Every Level III candidate sits the core topics above plus one pathway of their choice.

Portfolio Management pathway

30–35% of the exam

Active equity, yield-curve and credit strategy, LDI, trade execution.

  • Index-Based Equity Strategies
  • Active Equity Investing: Strategies
  • Active Equity Investing: Portfolio Construction
  • Liability-Driven and Index-Based Strategies
  • Yield Curve Strategies
  • Fixed-Income Active Management: Credit Strategies
  • Trade Strategy and Execution
  • Case Study in Portfolio Management: Institutional (Endowment)

Private Markets pathway

30–35% of the exam

GP/LP economics, private equity and debt, special situations, real assets.

  • Private Investments and Structures
  • General Partner and Investor Perspectives and the Investment Process
  • Private Equity
  • Private Debt
  • Private Special Situations
  • Private Real Estate Investments
  • Infrastructure

Private Wealth pathway

30–35% of the exam

Goals-based planning, tax efficiency, human capital, wealth transfer.

  • The Private Wealth Management Industry
  • Working With the Wealthy
  • Wealth Planning
  • Investment Planning
  • Preserving the Wealth
  • Advising the Wealthy
  • Transferring the Wealth
