About GroundLearn

GroundLearn is a small, independent studio that makes offline study apps for subjects that are hard to start. The first two apps are for finance.

What we make

GroundLearn builds study apps that teach the foundations first, then bring back what you miss. Cornerstone covers CFA and FRM exam preparation. OTC Learn covers over-the-counter derivatives. Both are free on Google Play, work offline, and show no ads.

More subjects will follow. The apps have no ads, no feeds, and no account is needed to start studying.

Who runs it

GroundLearn is run by one person who has worked in derivatives valuation and risk for many years. That background shapes the apps: the aim is to take a hard topic, break it into steps that fit into a day, and make sure what you learn stays learned.

Where to find us

  • Study pages, glossary and calculators on this site, free to read without installing anything.
  • Videos on YouTube.
  • Updates on the WhatsApp channels for Cornerstone and OTC Learn.
  • Questions, corrections and ideas: contactgroundlearn@gmail.com.
