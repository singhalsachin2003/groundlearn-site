How it works

Follow a red blood cell

  1. Your cell starts with no oxygen, coming back from the body.
  2. It enters the right side of the heart, which pumps it to the lungs.
  3. In the lungs it picks up oxygen, returns to the left side of the heart, and is pumped out to the body.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Your blood is a delivery service on a two-loop road: one loop goes to the lungs to pick up oxygen, the other carries it to the rest of the body, and the heart is the pump that keeps both loops moving.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The heart has two sides and pumps blood in two loops
  • How blood picks up oxygen in the lungs
  • What the main organs do
