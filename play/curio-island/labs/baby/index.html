How it works

Find the mum

  1. A baby animal is shown with its name.
  2. Tap the grown-up animal that is its mum.
  3. Wrong guesses just shake. Try another.

Explorer labs read instructions aloud, and a wrong try never costs anything.

Why it works

The big idea

Babies grow up to look like their parents, even when they look a bit different when they are small, like a tadpole and a frog.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Names of baby animals
  • Babies grow into grown-ups like their parents
  • Matching and sorting
