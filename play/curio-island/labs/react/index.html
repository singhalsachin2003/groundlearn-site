How it works

Make it fizz and test the gas

  1. Add baking soda to the beaker.
  2. Add vinegar. An acid and a base react and bubbles of gas rise.
  3. While it fizzes, press Limewater test. The limewater turns cloudy, so the gas is carbon dioxide.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A reaction is like a dance where partners swap: the atoms stay the same, but they leave one partner and hold hands with another, so you end up with brand-new substances.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Signs of a chemical reaction: gas, colour change, heat
  • Acids, bases and the limewater test for carbon dioxide
  • Atoms are rearranged in a reaction, not created or destroyed
