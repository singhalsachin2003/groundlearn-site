How it works

Build an animal cell

  1. Pick a part, such as Nucleus.
  2. Tap its job, such as Control centre. A right match joins your cell.
  3. Plant cells also have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a big vacuole.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A cell is a tiny factory: the nucleus is the office giving instructions, mitochondria are the power station, the membrane is the gate with guards, and a plant cell adds solar panels (chloroplasts) and a brick wall (cell wall).

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The parts of animal and plant cells
  • What the nucleus, membrane and mitochondria do
  • How microbes multiply and how we slow them down
