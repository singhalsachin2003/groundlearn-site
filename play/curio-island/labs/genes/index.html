How it works

Purple and white flowers

  1. A pea plant has two genes for colour. A big P (purple) beats a small p (white).
  2. Cross two Pp plants. The babies are PP, Pp, Pp and pp.
  3. Three are purple and one is white. White shows only when there is no big P.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Each parent passes on one of its two copies of a gene, like each parent putting one card into the baby's hand. A dominant card (big P) shows whenever it is there; a recessive one (small p) shows only when both cards are small.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Genes come in pairs, one from each parent
  • Dominant and recessive traits
  • Why two purple Pp parents give 3 purple to 1 white
