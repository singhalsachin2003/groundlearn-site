How it works

What is it made of?

  1. Read what to find.
  2. Tap every object that fits. Tap again to un-choose.
  3. Press "Check my choices". Different materials are good for different jobs.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Choosing a material is like choosing shoes: rain boots for puddles, sandals for the beach. A window is glass because it is see-through; a raincoat is plastic because it keeps water out.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Properties of materials
  • Matching materials to jobs
  • Sorting and checking choices
