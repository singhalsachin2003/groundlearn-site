How it works

Area and perimeter

  1. Tap squares on the grid to colour them.
  2. Area is how many squares you coloured. Perimeter is the length of the outside edge.
  3. Same area can have different perimeters: a long thin shape has a longer edge.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Area is how much carpet a room needs; perimeter is how much skirting board goes round the edge. Two rooms can need the same carpet but very different lengths of skirting.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Area as a count of unit squares
  • Perimeter as the distance round the edge
  • Shapes with the same area can have different perimeters
