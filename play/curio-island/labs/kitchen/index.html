How it works

Make a fizz and a colour change

  1. Tap Baking soda and Vinegar, then press Mix. They make carbon dioxide bubbles.
  2. Press Empty. Now tap Cabbage juice and Lemon juice and Mix: the juice turns pink, because lemon is an acid.
  3. You can guess the result before you mix. A wrong guess costs a heart, so it is optional.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Red-cabbage juice is a colour spy: it turns pink when it meets an acid like lemon and green-blue when it meets a base like baking soda. Fizz is a new gas being made, the same carbon dioxide that is in fizzy drinks.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Signs of a chemical change: fizz, colour, smell
  • Acids and bases at home
  • Making a prediction and testing it
