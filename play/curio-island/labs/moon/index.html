How it works

Phases of the Moon

  1. The Sun always lights half of the Moon.
  2. Slide the Moon round the Earth. From Earth you see more or less of the lit half.
  3. Moon between Earth and Sun: new moon. Earth between: full moon.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Hold a ball near a lamp and walk around it: one half of the ball is always lit, but how much of that bright half you see depends on where the ball is. The Moon is the ball and the Sun is the lamp.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The Moon does not make its own light
  • Phases come from how much of the lit half we see
  • Why the cycle takes about a month
