How it works

Build a meadow food chain

  1. Start with the one that makes its own food from sunlight: the grass.
  2. Who eats the grass? The rabbit.
  3. Who eats the rabbit? The fox. The energy goes along the arrows: Sun → grass → rabbit → fox.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A food chain is a relay race for energy: the Sun hands energy to the plant, the plant to the animal that eats it, and so on along the arrows.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Producers and consumers
  • Arrows show the direction energy flows
  • Herbivores, carnivores and predators
