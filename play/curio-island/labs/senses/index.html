How it works

Which sense is it?

  1. Pick a thing, then tap the sense that notices it.
  2. Eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, skin feels.
  3. We use our senses to learn about the world.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Your senses are your body's five reporters: eyes report what things look like, ears what they sound like, nose, tongue and skin how they smell, taste and feel. Your brain reads all the reports.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The five senses and their organs
  • Which sense notices which thing
  • Senses help us learn about the world
