How it works

Light the bulb

  1. The circuit has a gap, so the bulb is off.
  2. Try each item on the tray in the gap. A coin and a paper clip are metal, so they let electricity through.
  3. An eraser or a stick does not. Put a coin in the gap and the bulb lights.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Electricity in a circuit is like water in a loop of pipe: it only flows if the loop is complete. A switch is a tap, a battery is a pump, and a bulb glows when the flow runs through it.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • A circuit must be a complete loop
  • Conductors (metals) and insulators (wood, rubber, plastic)
  • Series and parallel circuits
