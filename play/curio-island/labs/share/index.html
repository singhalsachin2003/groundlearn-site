How it works

Share the sweets

  1. Tap a friend to give them one sweet from the pile.
  2. Keep going until the pile is empty.
  3. Everyone must end up with the same number. If not, it clears and you try again.

Explorer labs read instructions aloud, and a wrong try never costs anything.

Why it works

The big idea

Fair sharing is dealing cards: give one to each friend in turn, round and round, until the pile is empty.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Sharing equally
  • Early division
  • Checking that everyone has the same
