How it works

Acids, bases and pH

  1. The pH scale runs from 1 (strong acid, red) through 7 (neutral, green) to 13 (strong base, purple).
  2. Acid drops push the pH down. Base drops push it up.
  3. Add drops until the marker sits on the outlined target.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

pH is a thermometer for "sourness": low numbers are acids like lemon juice, 7 is neutral like pure water, high numbers are bases like soap. The indicator's colour is the reading on the thermometer.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The pH scale and neutral 7
  • Acids lower pH, bases raise it
  • Indicators show pH by colour
