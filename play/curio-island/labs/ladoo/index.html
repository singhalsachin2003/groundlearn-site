Ladoo Fractions

Take a fraction of a box of ladoos, such as 1/2 of 8 or 3/4 of 12, by tapping the right number of sweets.

How it works

A fraction of the box

  1. The box holds equal ladoos. 1/2 of 8 means split 8 into 2 equal parts and take 1 part.
  2. Tap the ladoos you take.
  3. Check: divide the box by the bottom number, then multiply by the top number.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

To find a fraction of a box, share the box between as many friends as the bottom number, then take as many friends' shares as the top number.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Fractions of a set
  • Dividing into equal parts
  • Finding 1/2, 1/3 and 3/4 of a number
