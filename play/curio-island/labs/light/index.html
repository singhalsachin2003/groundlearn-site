How it works

Turn a mirror to hit the crystal

  1. Tap a silver mirror to select it.
  2. Turn it in 15° steps. Turning a mirror by 15° swings the beam by 30°.
  3. Keep turning until the beam reaches the crystal and it lights up.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Light bounces off a mirror like a ball off a wall: it leaves at the same angle it arrived. Turn the mirror a little and the beam swings twice as far.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The law of reflection: angle in equals angle out
  • Turning a mirror by θ turns the reflected beam by 2θ
  • Light travels in straight lines
