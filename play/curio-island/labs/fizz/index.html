How it works

Make bubbles

  1. The picture at the top says what to make.
  2. Tap two things to drop into the bowl. Soap and water make bubbles.
  3. If nothing happens, the bowl clears and you try again. You cannot lose.

Explorer labs read instructions aloud, and a wrong try never costs anything.

Why it works

The big idea

Some things change when they meet, like soap meeting water to make bubbles, or vinegar meeting baking soda to make fizzy gas.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Mixing things can make something new
  • Watching closely and saying what happened
  • Trying again when nothing happens
