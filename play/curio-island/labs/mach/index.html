How it works

Lift a 600 N rock with a lever

  1. You can push down with 200 N, and the rock weighs 600 N.
  2. 600 ÷ 200 = 3, so your side of the plank must be at least 3 times longer than the rock's side.
  3. Slide the pivot close to the rock until that is true, then push.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Simple machines trade distance for force, like taking a long gentle path up a hill instead of a short steep one: you walk farther, but each step is easier.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Levers: effort × distance = load × distance
  • How pulleys and ramps reduce the force needed
  • Machines make work easier, not smaller
