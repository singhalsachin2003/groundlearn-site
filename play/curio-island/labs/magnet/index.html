How it works

What will the magnet pull?

  1. Tap every object you think is magnetic.
  2. Press "Test with the magnet". Green ones stuck, red ones did not.
  3. Magnets pull iron and steel. They ignore wood, plastic, glass, and even some metals like gold and copper.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A magnet is fussy about who it picks up: it grabs iron and steel (and nickel), but it ignores wood, plastic, glass and even metals like gold, copper and aluminium.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Magnetic and non-magnetic materials
  • Not all metals are magnetic
  • Testing a guess
