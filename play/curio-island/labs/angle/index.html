Angle Maker

How it works

Angles by eye

  1. Read the target, for example 90°.
  2. Turn the yellow arm until the opening looks right. No numbers to help!
  3. Press "Check my angle". You need to be within 6°. The green dashes show the true answer.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Angles are slices of a round cake: a right angle (90°) is a quarter of the cake, a straight line (180°) is half, and a full turn (360°) is the whole cake.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Acute, right, obtuse and reflex angles
  • Estimating angles by eye
  • A full turn is 360°
