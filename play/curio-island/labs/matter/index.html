How it works

Melt and boil water

  1. Slide the temperature up from -10 °C. Ice particles jiggle in place.
  2. Past 0 °C they slide past each other: liquid water.
  3. At 100 °C they fly apart: steam. Slide back down and it condenses.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Particles are like people in a room: when it is cold they stand still holding hands (solid), warmer and they shuffle about (liquid), hot enough and they run off in every direction (gas).

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Solid, liquid and gas in terms of particles
  • Melting at 0 °C and boiling at 100 °C (for water)
  • Condensing and freezing as the reverse changes
