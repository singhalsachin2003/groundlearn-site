How it works

Read the clock

  1. The short hand points to the hour.
  2. When the long hand points straight up at 12, it is exactly that hour o'clock.
  3. Tap the number that matches the short hand.

Explorer labs read instructions aloud, and a wrong try never costs anything.

Why it works

The big idea

A clock is like a race track with two runners: the short hand walks slowly and shows the hour; the long hand runs all the way round each hour.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Hour and minute hands
  • Telling the time to the hour
  • Numbers 1 to 12 on a clock face
