How it works

Three eighths

  1. The pizza is cut into 8 equal slices.
  2. 3/8 means 3 slices out of 8, so tap 3 slices.
  3. For a ratio such as 2 : 3, keep 2 parts red for every 3 parts blue. 4 : 6 works too.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A fraction is a pizza order: the bottom number says how many equal slices the pizza is cut into, the top says how many you take. A ratio is a recipe: 2 scoops of red for every 3 of blue, however big the batch.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Fractions as parts of a whole
  • Equivalent fractions and ratios (2 : 3 = 4 : 6)
  • Using ratio to scale a mixture
