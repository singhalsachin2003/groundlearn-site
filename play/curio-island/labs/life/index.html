How it works

From egg to grown-up

  1. The cards show the stages of a life, mixed up.
  2. Tap them in order, from the very start to the grown-up.
  3. A butterfly goes egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A life cycle is a circle, not a line: the grown-up makes eggs or seeds, and the story begins again.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Life cycles of insects, amphibians, birds and plants
  • Metamorphosis (caterpillar to butterfly, tadpole to frog)
  • Sequencing stages
