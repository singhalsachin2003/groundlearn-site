How it works

Tune a note

  1. Pitch is the frequency in hertz: waves per second. More waves means a higher note.
  2. Volume is the amplitude: how tall the wave is.
  3. Wavelength = 340 ÷ frequency, so 680 Hz gives 0.5 m.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

Sound is like ripples on a pond made by a tapping finger: tap faster and the ripples are closer together (higher pitch); tap harder and they are taller (louder).

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • Pitch depends on frequency (hertz)
  • Loudness depends on amplitude
  • Wavelength = speed of sound ÷ frequency
