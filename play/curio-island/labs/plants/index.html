How it works

What does each part do?

  1. Pick a part, then tap its job.
  2. Roots drink, stems carry, leaves make food, flowers make seeds.
  3. Every part helps the whole plant grow.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A plant is a team: roots are the straws that drink water, the stem is the lift that carries it up, leaves are the kitchens that make food from sunlight, and flowers make the seeds for new plants.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • The parts of a plant
  • What each part does
  • Plants make their own food from sunlight
