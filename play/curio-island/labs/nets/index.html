Cube Nets

How it works

Fold a cube

  1. A net is a flat pattern of 6 squares.
  2. Press Fold. Each square swings up on its edge like a little door.
  3. If the squares close into a cube with no gaps and no overlaps, it is a real cube net.

Stuck? Every level has a hint, and a "Show me" walk-through.

Why it works

The big idea

A cube net is a cardboard box that has been cut open and pressed flat. Fold the flaps back up like little doors, and the box closes only if no flaps overlap and none are missing.

What children learn

Skills in this lab

  • A cube has 6 faces
  • Which flat nets fold into a cube
  • Picturing 3D shapes from 2D drawings
