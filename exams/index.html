Dates and topic weights

  • CFA exam dates 2027Windows and registration deadlines for Levels I, II and III.
  • CFA Level I topic weights for 2027Multiple-choice questions over 4.5 hours, in two sessions of 135 minutes. Offered in February, May, August and November.
  • CFA Level II topic weights for 2027Vignette-based (item set) multiple-choice questions over 4.5 hours, in two sessions of 132 minutes. Offered in May, August and November.
  • CFA Level III topic weights for 2027Vignette-based essay and multiple-choice questions over 4.5 hours, in two sessions of 132 minutes, with one of three specialised pathways. Offered in February and August.
  • FRM exam dates 2027Windows, registration periods and fees for Parts I and II.
  • FRM Part I topic weights100 equally weighted multiple-choice questions in four hours, by computer-based testing. Offered in May, August and November.
  • FRM Part II topic weights80 equally weighted multiple-choice questions in four hours, by computer-based testing. Offered in May, August and November.
